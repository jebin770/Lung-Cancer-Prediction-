Methodology & Variables

The LungPredict screening framework compiles clinical risk coefficients derived from authoritative thoracic oncology datasets, including peer-reviewed papers from the National Lung Screening Trial (NLST). Inputs are parsed by our in-memory predictive engine to analyze relative risks across environmental, demographic, and behavioral health vectors.

Demographics

Age serves as an exponential risk multiplier in computational models. Clinical data indicates that somatic gene mutations and lung tissue vulnerabilities escalate significantly past age 45. Gender profiles calibrate baseline risk coefficients based on gender-stratified epidemiological surveys.

Primary Symptoms

Biomarkers like persistent coughing, wheezing, localized chest pain, chronic fatigue, and progressive shortness of breath (dyspnea) are calculated as heavy additive factor weights. The persistent presence of multiple symptoms triggers critical elevation thresholds.

Behavioral Indicators

Direct tobacco smoking and high alcohol intake are processed as synergistic mutational hazards. Chronic exposure to second-hand chemical smoke, occupational toxins (radon, asbestos), or industrial fine particulates amplifies bronchial cellular vulnerability indices.

🔐 Data Isolation Protocol: All values and screening inputs are processed purely inside your browser memory cache. No medical, identifiable, or telemetry data is transmitted to remote servers, aligning fully with HIPAA confidentiality boundaries.

Deep Breathing & Pranayama Protocols

  • Anulom Vilom (Alternate Nostril Breathing): Balances respiratory pathways. Perform for 10-15 minutes daily in clean ventilation.
  • Kapalbhati Pranayama: Aids in clearing active bronchial airways. Ideal for clearing mucus buildup. (Avoid if hypertensive).
  • Diaphragmatic Breathing: Place one hand on chest, one on stomach. Inhale for 4 seconds, expand belly, hold for 2, exhale slowly for 6.

Nutrition & Lung Cellular Recovery

  • Antioxidant Rich Foods: Load up on fresh citrus fruits, berries, and raw turmeric (curcumin reduces bronchial inflammation).
  • Cruciferous Veggies: Broccoli, cabbage, and cauliflower contain high glucosinolates, shielding tissue from carcinogenic mutations.
  • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Supplement with flaxseeds, walnuts, or clean fish oils to actively decrease systemic respiratory inflammation.

Air Pollution Shielding (Indoor / Outdoor)

  • HEPA Filtration: Install a True-HEPA air purifier inside bedrooms to capture 99.97% of fine respiratory particulates (PM2.5).
  • Indoor Botanicals: Snake plants, Spider plants, and Peace lilies naturally filter airborne benzene and formaldehydes.
  • CPCB Monitoring: Check the local Air Quality Index (AQI) daily. Avoid outdoor conditioning if AQI exceeds 150.

Active Cardio Conditioning

  • Brisk Walking: 30 minutes of daily brisk aerobic activity stimulates active alveoli expansion and carbon dioxide clearing.
  • Thoracic Stretching: Interlock hands behind back, pull shoulders down, and expand chest to improve structural vital capacity.
  • Interval Conditioning: Engage in light interval cycling or swimming to naturally elevate peak aerobic oxygen absorption.
